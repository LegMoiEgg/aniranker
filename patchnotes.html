v9.0 - 17.07.2026

NEW FEATURE – CONNECTION

- New game mode: Connection — find 4 groups of 4 characters that share a common trait

- Groups are generated from shared anime, hair color, eye color, or genre

- 4 lives (hearts) — lose one for each wrong guess

- Solved groups appear at the top with color-coded labels and character images

- Game state saved in Local Storage (resume where you left off)

- Ambiguity check ensures no group can be confused with another valid group

- Added eye color to all characters (used in Connection and Lexikon)

- Main menu grid changed from 2 columns to 4 columns for a wider layout

LEXIKON

A brand-new overview of all characters in the game.

Browse the full roster as a grid — sorted alphabetically.

Click on any character to see their full profile: Anime, Genre, Gender, Age, Height and Haircolor.

Accessible from the main menu.

ANIDLE IMPROVEMENTS

- New attribute: Anime Genre — each character now has genre tags for their anime (e.g. Action / Fantasy / Isekai)

- Partial match: if a guessed value shares at least one part with the correct value, the cell turns orange instead of red

(e.g. guessing a character with "Pink / Green" hair when the answer is "Pink" → orange)

- Hint system: after 5, 10 and 15 guesses, hints unlock one by one

Hint 1 (after 5): Genre  |  Hint 2 (after 10): Haircolor  |  Hint 3 (after 15): Anime

- Hints must be clicked to reveal — they don't spoil automatically

- Win screen now shows how many tries it took to guess the character

NEW GAME MODE: ANIDLE

Think you know your anime characters? Prove it.

A random character is chosen from the roster. Your job: figure out who it is.

Type any character name into the search field and submit your guess.

Each guess reveals how close you are — attribute by attribute:

- ✅ Green: exact match

- ❌ Red: no match

- ↑ Arrow up: the correct value is higher

- ↓ Arrow down: the correct value is lower

Attributes compared: Anime, Gender, Age, Height and Haircolor.

Your guesses stack up — newest on top. Use the clues wisely.

Guess the character before you run out of ideas.

Hit "New Game" after a win to start over with a new mystery character.

More updates and characters coming soon. Stay tuned.
